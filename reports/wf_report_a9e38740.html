Statistical Appendix & Metric Definitions

Interquartile Range (IQR) Outlier Rule
Lower Bound = Q1 - (1.5 × IQR); Upper Bound = Q3 + (1.5 × IQR)
Identifies values that fall beyond 1.5 times the spread of the middle 50% of the distribution. Established by John Tukey for robust outlier detection resistant to extreme values.
Pearson Correlation Coefficient (r)
r = Σ((x - x̄)(y - ȳ)) / (√Σ(x - x̄)² × √Σ(y - ȳ)²)
Measures linear relationship strength between two continuous variables, bounded between -1.0 (perfect negative) and +1.0 (perfect positive). Does NOT imply causal relationship.
Completeness Score (%)
(1 - (Total Null Cells / Total Cell Count)) × 100%
Measures overall dataset fill rate across all recorded rows and columns. Scores above 95% indicate high data completeness.
